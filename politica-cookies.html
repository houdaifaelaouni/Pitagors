Cookie Policy

In compliance with Law 34/2002 of 11 July on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI-CE), and with the guidance of the Spanish Data Protection Authority (AEPD), Montajes Pitagor S.L. informs you about the cookies used on this website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser when you visit it. They serve various purposes, from remembering your preferences to tracking your behaviour for advertising.

2. Cookies used on this website

This website does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. Specifically:

  • No third-party analytics scripts (e.g. Google Analytics) are loaded.
  • No advertising pixels or retargeting scripts are present.
  • No social-network widgets are embedded on the page.

The only technical element that may set a cookie is the language preference you select (English / Spanish). This is a strictly necessary functional cookie, stored only in your browser's local storage, and does not identify you or leave the device.

3. Performance monitoring

Hosting is provided by Vercel Inc. Vercel collects standard server request logs (IP address, browser type, page requested, timestamp) for operational purposes such as security and performance. These logs are not used to track individual users across sessions and are not associated with personally identifiable information by Montajes Pitagor S.L. Vercel's privacy practices are described in the Vercel Privacy Policy.

4. Enlaces externos

This website contains a link to open WhatsApp (wa.me). Clicking it opens the WhatsApp application, which is operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. and subject to its own cookie and privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for cookies set by WhatsApp or Meta.

5. How to manage cookies

Since this site does not set any non-essential cookies, no cookie consent banner is required. If you wish to clear or block all cookies, you can do so through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if the technical setup of the website changes. The date at the bottom of this page indicates when it was last updated.

7. Contact

For any question about this Cookie Policy, contact us at montajes@pitagor.info.
