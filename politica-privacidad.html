2. Personal data we collect and how

This website does not collect personal data through forms or tracking systems. The only way we may receive your personal data is if you contact us directly:

  • By email: to montajes@pitagor.info. We receive your email address and the content you write.
  • By WhatsApp: using the link on this site, which opens the WhatsApp application (a service operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd). Your conversation is conducted directly with us through WhatsApp. The data you share in that conversation is processed under WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Montajes Pitagor S.L. stores those messages on the device used for business communication.
  • By phone: we may note your phone number and the subject of the call.

PurposeLegal basis

4. Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as the business relationship is active or for as long as required by applicable law (generally 5 years for accounting records, 6 years for commercial correspondence). Data collected solely to respond to an enquiry that does not result in a business relationship is deleted within 1 year of the last communication.

5. Data transfers

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. Data may be shared with:

  • Public authorities, when legally required.
  • WhatsApp / Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., if you choose to contact us through WhatsApp. See Meta's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Hosting provider Vercel Inc. (United States), which processes server request logs on our behalf and is subject to EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the LOPDGDD, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure when the data is no longer necessary.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Data portability (where technically feasible).

To exercise these rights, write to us at montajes@pitagor.info or at our registered address, enclosing a copy of an identity document. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Authority (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.

7. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk in order to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access.
