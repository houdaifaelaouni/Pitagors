The project

A warehouse that is its own building

The client needed high-density storage on a site where a conventional building was not an option. In a self-supporting warehouse the racking carries the roof, so the storage and the structure are the same installation. That means every upright has to be plumb, braced and torqued exactly to the manufacturer's plan, because nothing else holds the building up.

Our crew worked in phases so the client could start receiving stock before the last bay was closed, and the site was handed over with the levels checked and the tightening documented.

  • Structure and uprightsUprights raised, plumbed and braced in the manufacturer's sequence.
  • Beams and pallet levelsBeams set level by level, with the safety pins and locking plates checked at each one.
  • Roof and claddingRoof and side panels fitted once the structure was signed off.
  • Checks and handoverLevels, tightening and damage inspected, then handed over with the documents the site required.
