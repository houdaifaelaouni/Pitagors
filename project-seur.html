The project

Several picking levels, one steel structure

Our crew installed a multi-level pick module for SEUR in Portugal: a steel structure with walkway floors on several levels and carton flow racking, where orders are picked by hand and cartons roll to the pick face.

We worked for Icon, the system provider, for eleven months in 2025, with a crew of 25 installers.
