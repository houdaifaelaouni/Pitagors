The project

A high-bay warehouse where the racking carries the building

Our crew installed the steel structure of a self-supporting high-bay warehouse for Inditex in Belgium. In this type of building the racking itself carries the roof and the walls, so every frame has to be set out, plumbed and anchored with precision.

We worked for Noega Systems, the system provider, for eight months in 2023, with a crew of 23 installers, mobile cranes and lifting platforms on an open site.
