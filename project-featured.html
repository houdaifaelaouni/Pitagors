The project

Racking installed inside a working building

The storage area sits inside an existing warehouse, so the crew worked under a roof that was already in place, with cable trays, sprinkler lines and lighting overhead. Each bay was set out and levelled against the building, and the layout had to clear the services above.

Installation went in phases so the client could keep receiving stock, and the site was handed over with the levels and tightening checked and documented.
