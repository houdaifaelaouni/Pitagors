FAQ

Questions before you write to us

Not here? Call or write: we answer in English or Spanish.

What do you need to give me a quote?

The plans or layout, the type of system, the country of the project and the date you need us. If you do not have everything yet, send what you have and we will help.

Which systems do you install?

Conventional, dynamic, compact, shuttle and mobile base racking, self-supporting warehouses and mezzanines. We also do maintenance and repair.

Do you work outside Spain?

Yes. We are based near Madrid and our crews are available for projects across Europe.

In which languages can we talk?

English and Spanish. For English enquiries call +34 670 517 273.

What about safety and documents?

We work at height following safety rules and can provide the documents your site requires. Tell us what your site asks for when you request the quote.

How fast can you start?

It depends on our schedule. Send us the date you need and we confirm availability in the quote.
